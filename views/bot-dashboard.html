Channel points

Timeout reward

A viewer redeems a channel-point reward and names a user, and the bot times that user out. Needs your Twitch account connected above.

With no reward chosen, any reward whose title contains timeout, ban, silence, mute or penalty counts.

Restricted mode

Only you, the bot owner and the users below can time people out. Everyone else just loses their points.
